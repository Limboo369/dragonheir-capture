What the numbers rest on

Hero stats, skill numbers, artifacts and sets come from the official game tables. Charge times, Skill Haste, the Accuracy curve and buff values come from sourced game rules. The DEF curve, gear, animations and boss stats are guesses. Move a slider and rerun: if a verdict holds across the whole range, it does not depend on the guess.
